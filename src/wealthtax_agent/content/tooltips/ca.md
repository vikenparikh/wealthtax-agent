# Canada wizard tooltips

Each H2 is a `field_key`. Body text is the tooltip rendered next to the
wizard field. Keep entries plain-English and CRA-accurate.

## rrsp_contribution
Money you put into a Registered Retirement Savings Plan (RRSP) reduces your
taxable income for the year. Your deduction is limited by your annual RRSP
contribution room (shown on your most recent Notice of Assessment). Contributions
made in the first 60 days of the following year can be deducted on either year's
return — choose the one that saves more tax.

## employment_income
Total employment income from Box 14 of every T4 slip you received this year.
This is gross pay before deductions but excludes employer-paid benefits.

## tuition_credit
Eligible tuition paid to a designated educational institution (T2202 Box 23).
The credit is non-refundable: it reduces tax owing but does not generate a refund
on its own. Unused amounts can be carried forward indefinitely or transferred to
a spouse/parent (up to $5,000 per year combined).

## medical_expenses
Eligible medical expenses you, your spouse, or your dependants paid during any
12-month period ending in the tax year. Only the amount above the lesser of
3% of net income or $2,759 (2025) can be claimed.

## charitable_donations
Donations to registered Canadian charities. First $200 generates a 15% federal
credit; amounts above $200 generate 29% (or 33% for income over the top bracket).
You can carry unused donations forward up to 5 years.

## fhsa_contribution
First Home Savings Account contributions are deductible like RRSPs, up to
$8,000/year and $40,000 lifetime. Withdrawals for a qualifying home purchase
are tax-free.

## property_tax_paid
Property tax paid on your principal residence. Renters can claim rent paid
(certain provinces only — Ontario Trillium Benefit, Manitoba Education
Property Tax Credit). Maximum credit is $12,000 of eligible expense.

## cpp_contributions
Canada Pension Plan contributions deducted from your pay (T4 Box 16). Most are
non-refundable credits; the "enhanced" portion (since 2019) is a deduction.

## ei_premiums
Employment Insurance premiums withheld at source (T4 Box 18). Non-refundable
federal/provincial credit.

## capital_gains
Net capital gains realized on the disposition of property (T5008 + manually
reported). Only 50% of net capital gains are taxable. Capital losses can only
offset capital gains, but unused losses carry forward indefinitely.

## foreign_tax_credit
Tax paid to a foreign government on foreign-source income can be credited
against Canadian tax on the same income. Calculated per country, capped at the
Canadian tax that would otherwise apply to that income.
