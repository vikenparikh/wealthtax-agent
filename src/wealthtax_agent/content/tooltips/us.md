# United States wizard tooltips

Each H2 is a `field_key`. Body text is the tooltip rendered next to the
wizard field. Keep entries plain-English and IRS-accurate.

## wages
Total wages, tips, and other compensation from Box 1 of every W-2 you received.
This is gross pay before federal income tax withholding but after pre-tax
deductions like 401(k) contributions.

## foreign_tax_credit
Tax paid to a foreign country on foreign-source income may be claimed as a
credit on Form 1116 (or directly on Schedule 3 if the total is under $300/$600
single/MFJ). The credit is limited to the U.S. tax that would otherwise apply
to that foreign-source income.

## student_loan_interest
Interest paid on a qualified student loan, reported on Form 1098-E Box 1.
Above-the-line deduction up to $2,500. Phases out at higher modified AGI.
Cross-border note: this expense can only be claimed in one country.

## ira_contribution
Traditional IRA contributions reduce your taxable income up to the annual
limit ($7,000 in 2025; $8,000 if age 50+). Deductibility phases out at higher
income if you (or your spouse) are covered by a workplace retirement plan.

## hsa_contribution
Health Savings Account contributions are above-the-line deductions. Limits:
$4,300 self-only / $8,550 family in 2025; +$1,000 catch-up at 55. Requires
enrollment in a high-deductible health plan.

## state_local_property_tax
Real-estate taxes paid on your home. Combined with state income/sales tax, the
total SALT deduction is capped at $10,000 per return ($5,000 MFS). Itemized
deduction on Schedule A.

## mortgage_interest
Interest paid on a qualified home loan secured by your primary or second home.
Reported on Form 1098 Box 1. Deductible on debt up to $750,000 (post-Dec 2017
acquisition debt) or $1,000,000 (pre-Dec 2017).

## qualified_dividends
Dividends from U.S. and qualifying foreign corporations, held for the required
period. Taxed at long-term capital gains rates (0% / 15% / 20% depending on
total income), not ordinary rates.

## charitable_contributions
Cash gifts to qualified U.S. 501(c)(3) charities (and certain foreign
equivalents). Itemized deduction limited to 60% of AGI for cash gifts to public
charities. Non-cash gifts have lower limits.

## social_security_wages
Wages subject to Social Security tax, reported on W-2 Box 3. Capped at the
annual wage base ($168,600 in 2024; $176,100 in 2025). Used to compute the
FICA portion of payroll tax.

## medicare_wages
Wages subject to Medicare tax, reported on W-2 Box 5. Uncapped; the additional
0.9% kicks in above $200k/$250k single/MFJ.

## standard_deduction
Default deduction available without itemising. 2025 amounts: $15,000 single,
$30,000 MFJ. Itemise only when total Schedule A deductions exceed the standard
deduction.
