# India wizard tooltips

Each H2 is a `field_key`. Body text is the tooltip rendered next to the
wizard field. Keep entries plain-English and current with India's old + new
regime rules.

## hra_exemption
House Rent Allowance exemption under section 10(13A). Lowest of three:
(a) actual HRA received, (b) 50% of basic salary (metro) or 40% (non-metro),
(c) rent paid minus 10% of basic salary. Available only under the old regime.

## section_80c
Eligible investments and expenses under section 80C: EPF, PPF, ELSS, LIC
premium, principal repayment on home loan, NSC, ULIP, tax-saving FD, tuition
fees for up to 2 children. Aggregate cap of ₹1,50,000. Only available under
the old regime.

## section_80d
Health insurance premium under section 80D. Up to ₹25,000 for self/spouse/
children, plus ₹25,000 for parents (₹50,000 if either parent is 60+).
Preventive health check-up sub-limit of ₹5,000 within the overall cap.

## standard_deduction_salary
Flat deduction from salary income. ₹50,000 under the old regime and ₹75,000
under the new regime (FY 2024-25 onwards). No documentation needed.

## section_87a_rebate
Rebate of up to ₹12,500 (old regime, income up to ₹5L) or ₹25,000 (new
regime, income up to ₹7L) under section 87A. Reduces tax payable to zero
within the threshold; phases off cleanly above it.

## ltcg_equity
Long-term capital gains on listed equity / equity mutual funds (held > 12
months). Pre-23-Jul-2024: 10% above ₹1 lakh exemption. Post-23-Jul-2024:
12.5% above ₹1.25 lakh exemption.

## stcg_equity
Short-term capital gains on listed equity / equity mutual funds (held ≤ 12
months). Pre-23-Jul-2024: 15%. Post-23-Jul-2024: 20%. Cess and surcharge
apply.

## section_24b_home_loan_interest
Interest on home loan deductible under section 24(b). Up to ₹2,00,000 for
self-occupied property; full amount for let-out property. Only available
under the old regime.

## tds_deducted
Tax Deducted at Source already withheld by employer/bank. Reported on Form
16 (salary), Form 16A (other income), 26AS, and AIS. Reduces final tax
liability; refunded if it exceeds tax owed.

## surcharge
Additional levy on tax for income above ₹50L. Rates: 10% (₹50L-₹1Cr),
15% (₹1Cr-₹2Cr), 25% (₹2Cr-₹5Cr), 37% (>₹5Cr — capped at 25% under the
new regime). Marginal relief applies near thresholds.

## health_education_cess
4% cess applied to the total of income tax plus surcharge. No threshold;
applies to all taxpayers.

## new_regime_choice
The default regime since AY 2024-25 is the new regime with lower rates but
fewer deductions (no 80C, 80D, HRA, etc.). Salaried taxpayers can opt out
each year; business income filers can opt out only once.
